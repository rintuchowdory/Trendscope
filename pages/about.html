About Us

Empowering Decisions with Data-Driven Insight

We believe that every team deserves access to real-time market intelligence — not just Fortune 500 companies with dedicated research departments.

Our Mission

TrendScope was founded in 2024 with a bold vision: to democratize trend analytics and make real-time market intelligence accessible to every organization, regardless of size or budget.

We aggregate data from over 10 million sources across the web — social media, news outlets, academic publications, forums, patent filings, and more — and transform that raw data into actionable insights using cutting-edge machine learning.
