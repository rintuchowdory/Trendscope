FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?
Our free plan is available indefinitely with up to 5 trend monitors. When you upgrade to Professional, you get a 14-day free trial with full access. Cancel anytime with no charges.
What data sources does TrendScope monitor?
We monitor 10M+ sources including Twitter/X, Reddit, news outlets, Google Trends, patent filings, academic papers, YouTube, TikTok, and more. Enterprise plans can add custom data sources.
Can I integrate TrendScope with my existing tools?
Yes! We offer native integrations with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zapier, webhooks, and a REST API. Professional plans include 10,000 API calls/month; Enterprise plans offer unlimited access.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Enterprise plans offer on-premise deployment and custom security configurations.
How accurate are the AI predictions?
Our prediction models have an average accuracy of 87% for 7-day forecasts and 74% for 30-day forecasts across all tracked trends. We continuously train and validate our models against real outcomes.
